# Code of conduct

Storied welcomes people with different backgrounds, skills, identities, and ways of creating. Treat contributors and users with patience and respect. Critique the work clearly; do not attack the person.

Harassment, discriminatory language, sexual attention without consent, threats, doxxing, impersonation, and publishing private creative work without permission are not acceptable. Do not pressure someone to disclose personal information or explain their identity. Respect requests to stop an interaction.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, limit participation, or exclude participants to protect the community. Decisions should be proportionate and explained where doing so does not expose private information. Reports are handled with discretion and shared only as needed to respond.

Report conduct concerns through a maintainer’s private contact channel listed on the repository profile. If none is available, open a minimal issue asking for a private reporting channel without posting identifying details. Do not publish allegations or private evidence in a public issue.

This policy applies to the repository, its issue and review discussions, and project spaces maintained under the Storied name.
