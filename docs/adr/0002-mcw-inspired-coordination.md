## Canon and application records

Canonical MCW concerns the emergent, dynamically maintained coupling of meaning, salience, intent, and continuity between HCW and ACW. It is not a transcript, database, memory, context buffer, vector retrieval system, UI, prompt, or a model feature. HCW is not enumerable and can advance spontaneously; ACW advances on input, including tool/background input. An IU is coordination-relevant transferable information; it is not equivalent to a token, fact, message, vector, or isolated belief. IU individuation remains open. These boundaries follow the pinned glossary and Constitution and are not redefined here.

`CoordinationState`, operational IU records, boundary records, workflow checkpoints, repair routing, and the UI are **MCW-inspired application extensions**. They preserve externalized information and support coordination practices; they are not literally or completely MCW, do not enumerate human cognition, and do not measure shared understanding. The application does not claim that a model has an MCW capability or that Storied implements MCW. A database record cannot prove that the human and AI understand the same thing.

The stored operational IU records use author-provided goals, constraints, priorities, intent, and corrections, with source boundary IDs and supersession. They are a pragmatic application representation, not a segmentation or counting instrument. Interpretations remain separate from exact source communication. An explicit choice can resolve a competing interpretation, but absence of objection is not treated as agreement.

## Repair operations

The canonical five remain **Re-grounding, Decompression, Re-weighting, Disambiguation, Synchronization**. Runtime operation identifiers are corresponding application labels. Synchronization is exercised by stale world/direction recovery; disambiguation records the human's intended meaning; re-grounding adds a source-linked correction without silently dropping constraints. Re-weighting is available when a critical context item cannot fit: the author must shorten/reprioritize directions or adjust the source, and an unchanged over-budget constraint still interrupts. Decompression's source recovery is supported by exact boundary records and summary source IDs; a dedicated interactive decompression editor is evolutionary work, not claimed as a complete implementation.

The upstream mapping explicitly identifies gaps for Constraint Opacity and Repair Suppression. Its proposed Constraint Disclosure and Repair-Norm Restoration are declared extensions, not additions to the canonical five. Storied's capability disclosure and non-suppressible routing are application mechanisms; we do not silently rename them canonical Re-weighting. An explicit retry acknowledges a capability failure; it is not evidence that shared understanding was restored.

A static prompt can establish initial directions but cannot observe a later edit, persist the communication boundary, block a stale approval, or route an interrupt. Those behaviors therefore reside in runtime application code. Source-based repair never asks a model to fabricate missing detail or private chain-of-thought. The inspector exposes application sources, filters, authority, versions, outcomes, and repairs only.

## Evidence and extension discipline

The framework is exploratory; its current empirical claims remain L0–L1 as stated by the Constitution. H/R/D/M are unvalidated ordinal research observables, not interval-scale performance measures. Storied implements none of them and produces no MCW score, percentage, health gauge, or quantitative coordination claim. Passing software tests demonstrates routing, persistence, and data invariants only; it does not validate MCW or prove reduced coordination failure.

Plain-language extension rationale: keep what the author actually said, show what the application inferred, and stop to resynchronize when those inputs change. This preserves rather than replaces the canonical vocabulary. Engineering falsification conditions are concrete: if a stale checkpoint can approve canon without review, a repair signal can be bypassed, or an interpretation overwrites its exact source, the corresponding mechanism has failed. The broader hypothesis that these mechanisms help people coordinate remains untested. A comparative study showing no benefit, or excessive interruptions without better outcomes, would count against that application hypothesis; tests here do not substitute for such a study.

No AI↔AI MCW claim is made. Future specialized-agent coordination must be separately declared an **MCW-inspired multi-agent extension** with its own scope and falsification conditions. The world and execution graph correctness mechanisms are ordinary software engineering; MCW is the source of coordination design guidance, not a claimed theory of database consistency, security, or model capability.
